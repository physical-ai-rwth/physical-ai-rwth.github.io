## Your responsibilities

Research on learning-based robotic systems that perceive, reason and act in complex
environments. Possible topics include vision-language-action models, robotic world models,
active perception, structured memory systems, human-robot cognitive alignment, hybrid
imitation and reinforcement learning, and medical robotics.

Your work will include developing methods, implementing and evaluating models, running
experiments in simulation and with physical robots, publishing at top international
venues, and contributing to the development of the group.

## Your profile

- An excellent master's degree in computer science, electrical engineering, robotics, or a
  related field
- Solid knowledge of at least one — ideally several — of machine learning, computer vision,
  language models, robotics, diffusion models, or reinforcement learning
- Strong programming skills, with the ability to rapidly prototype, implement and evaluate
  research ideas, preferably in Python and PyTorch
- High motivation, scientific curiosity, and the ability to work both independently and
  collaboratively
- Excellent communication skills in English; German is advantageous but not required

## What we offer

- The opportunity to join and shape a young and growing research group
- Close supervision and scientific mentoring, with support for publications, conference
  participation and academic career development
- Access to the newly built NEURA Gym RWTH Aachen, with robots operating in real-world
  scenarios
- Extensive on-premises computing infrastructure including around 120 GPUs, plus
  additional high-end GPU clusters on demand
- Active collaborations with partners from academia and industry
- A full-time position at pay grade EG 13 TV-L

## Application

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Please include a CV, academic transcripts, a
statement of research interests, and, if available, links to publications, thesis work, or
code repositories.
