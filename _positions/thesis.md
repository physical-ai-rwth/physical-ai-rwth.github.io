## Scope

Bachelor's and master's thesis projects across the group's research areas —
vision-language-action models, world models, active perception, structured memory,
imitation and reinforcement learning, and medical robotics.

Topics are shaped around your interests and our current directions rather than picked from
a fixed list, so get in touch with a short note on what you would like to work on.

## Your profile

- Enrolled in a relevant master's or bachelor's programme
- Some background in machine learning, computer vision or robotics
- Programming experience, preferably Python and PyTorch

## Application

Send a short email with your CV, your transcript, and a paragraph on the direction that
interests you. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
