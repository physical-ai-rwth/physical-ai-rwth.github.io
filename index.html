What we do

We develop generalist robots that autonomously solve real-world long-horizon tasks.

The Physical AI Lab is a young and growing research group at the Chair of Imaging and Computer Vision, RWTH Aachen University. We build learning-based robotic systems that perceive, reason and act in complex environments — combining vision, language and control into agents that carry a task through to completion rather than executing a single isolated skill.

Our work spans method development, large-scale training, and evaluation both in simulation and on physical robots operating in real-world scenarios.
