For a robot to be a useful collaborator, its understanding of a task has to line up with
the person's. We investigate how to align robot representations, intentions and
explanations with human expectations, so instructions are interpreted as they were meant
and the robot's behaviour stays legible.

Legibility matters in both directions: the person should be able to predict the robot, and
the robot should notice when it has misread the person.
