Perception is not passive. We study how robots should move sensors and bodies to gather
the information a task actually requires — deciding where to look, when to look again, and
how to resolve ambiguity through deliberate interaction with the environment.

This turns perception into a decision problem: the robot must estimate what it does not
yet know, and judge whether finding out is worth the time it costs.
