Working within a chair with deep expertise in imaging and medical technology, we explore
how learned robotic autonomy transfers to clinical settings, where precision, reliability
and interpretability carry particular weight.

Clinical deployment raises the bar on every claim: a policy that works most of the time is
not a policy that can be used.
