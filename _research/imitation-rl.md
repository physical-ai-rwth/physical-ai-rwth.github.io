Demonstrations give a strong starting point; reinforcement learning refines it. We combine
the two so that robots learn quickly from human data and then improve through their own
experience, with an emphasis on sample efficiency and on transferring policies from
simulation to physical hardware.

The interesting difficulty is knowing when to stop trusting the demonstration — human data
is a prior, not a ceiling.
