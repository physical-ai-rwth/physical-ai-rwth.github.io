Yuli Wu leads the Physical AI Lab at the Chair of Imaging and Computer Vision, RWTH Aachen
University. The group develops generalist robots that autonomously solve real-world,
long-horizon tasks.

His research centres on learning-based robotic systems that perceive, reason and act in
complex environments — connecting vision, language and control so that a robot can carry a
task through to completion rather than executing a single isolated skill.

The group is recruiting doctoral researchers, and welcomes enquiries from prospective
HiWis and thesis students.
